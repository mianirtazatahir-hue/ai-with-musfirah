📊 Understanding CPM vs RPM:

CPM (Cost Per Mille): What advertisers pay per 1000 impressions (before YouTube cut)

RPM (Revenue Per Mille): What you actually earn per 1000 views (after YouTube's 45% cut)

Rough Formula: RPM = CPM × 0.55 (YouTube takes 45%, you get 55%)
